Why this matters

Every leader builds something that outlasts them

Almost none of it is ever written down.

The lessons, the turning points, the philosophy earned over decades — they live in one mind, and then they are gone. We exist to make sure they are not.

The core promise
“We help leaders, founders and families intentionally preserve their stories, values and contributions — for the generations who come after.”
We don’t sell videos or books. We preserve the people behind them.

“A man cannot command the weather. He can only decide how he will meet it.”

46
Ridgeway, 1971

In 1971 the long rains did not come. My father walked the rows every morning, as if looking could make the clouds gather. The coffee flowered and then dropped its flowers, and the river by the school became a path of stones.

That was the year I learned what a cooperative is for. We dug the first water pans by hand: eleven families, one borrowed wheelbarrow and a great deal of singing. Nobody was paid. Everybody was fed.

47
